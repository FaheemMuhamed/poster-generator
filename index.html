What is Morva?

Morva is a free, browser-based archive of 56 generative visuals made with p5.js, three.js and GLSL. Designers can tweak each one without code, add type, and export it as a poster, a video clip or code.

Is Morva free?

Yes. Morva is free to use with no account. An optional Support button on Gumroad helps it grow.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Every visual has plain controls for its shape, form, motion, colour, type and finish. The code is there if you want it.

What can I export?

A PNG up to 6K (6144 px), a video clip, an embed iframe, a standalone HTML file, or runnable p5.js, three.js or GLSL code with your settings built in.

Can I use my own image or webcam?

Yes. Image-based effects can use your own photo or your webcam. It stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Which parameters can I change?

Each visual has its own parameters, such as density, flow, speed and distortion, plus how it reacts to your cursor and clicks, three one-click looks, and a finish such as grain, bloom, halftone, riso, glitch or pixel.
